# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Practicing lawyers — attorneys, paralegals, and clerks checking doctrine and
pulling authorities for real matters. They arrive with a live question, read in
sustained stretches, verify before they rely, and they print.

## Product Purpose

digest.law publishes the American Legal Digest: open, source-grounded digests
of United States legal doctrine, machine-researched from free public sources,
review-gated, and shipped with every retained source and every search log
attached. Success is being trusted and cited — practitioners and scholars treat
digests as a credible starting point and link to them.

## Positioning

A free, open-access digest of American legal doctrine — no paywall, no login,
machine-readable.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: search a topic — a visitor arrives with a question and goes
  straight to search.
- Secondary CTA: open a sample digest — a curated exemplar that shows the
  format before the visitor has a question of their own.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "A free digest of American
  legal doctrine, with the evidence attached."
- Belief ladder: (1) it's free and covers real doctrine; (2) digests come from
  actual authorities, not AI invention — the evidence is attached; (3) I can
  verify any claim in seconds via the attached sources and audit trail.
- Proof on hand: the corpus itself — every digest's audit trail, retained
  sources, and the methodology page. No testimonials or press yet.

## Brand Personality

Plainspoken, rigorous, open. Public-good energy: direct language, the method
up front, welcoming to non-specialists without diluting precision. The site
speaks like a careful editor, never like a vendor.

## Anti-references

- Legal-tech SaaS: gradient heroes, feature cards, pricing-page energy,
  "AI-powered" badges.
- Government portal: GPO / uscourts.gov blandness — default fonts,
  seal-and-banner officialdom.
- AI-startup aesthetic: dark-mode-by-default, glassmorphism, sparkle icons,
  chat-first framing.

## Design Principles

1. Show the evidence, don't claim it — every trust statement on any page
   resolves to a checkable artifact: a retained source, an audit entry, a
   search log. Nothing asks to be believed on assertion.
2. Free means frictionless — no logins, banners, interstitials, or upsells;
   the path from question to doctrine stays unobstructed.
3. Plainspoken over impressive — say what a thing is in direct language; no
   salesmanship, no AI spectacle, no euphemism for machine authorship.
4. Reading is the product — the digest page carries the identity; chrome stays
   quiet and nothing competes with sustained reading.
5. Honest about absence — empty indexes, small areas, and failed conversions
   are presented as documented results, never hidden or padded.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA, plus a first-class print commitment: lawyers print, and a printed
digest must be flawless — the print stylesheet is a primary surface, not an
afterthought. Body contrast ≥ 4.5:1, visible focus states, and reduced-motion
alternatives for every animation.
