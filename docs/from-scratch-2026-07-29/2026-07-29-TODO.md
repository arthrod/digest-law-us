## Scenario and rules

This ledger assumes:

- no candidate concept has been accepted into the new registry;
- no new digest article has been generated;
- no new release has been published; and
- existing code/corpus may be reused only after the relevant greenfield
  acceptance test passes.

The only statuses are **DONE** and **PENDING**.

- **DONE** means the exact decision, evidence-collection task, or reusable
  prototype claim in the row is complete.
- **PENDING** means the greenfield implementation or acceptance test is not
  complete.

No row combines a completed subtask with an uncompleted one. When implementation
and qualification differ, they are separate rows.

## Program objective

Build an open legal issue digest whose conservative curation, source discipline,
and lawyer usefulness can credibly compete with professional digest systems,
while making the methodology auditable and reproducible across jurisdictions
and languages.

The direct digest benchmarks are the West Topic and Key Number/West Digest
function and LexisNexis headnotes. KeyCite and Shepard's are citator benchmarks.
Citator claims remain pending until the separate authority-treatment workstream
passes.
