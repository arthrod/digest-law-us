American Legal Digest — United States

Open, source-grounded digests of U.S. legal issues: 1,621 digest bundles across 42 areas of U.S. law, every bundle resting on at least 2 retained public-source documents with a full audit trail. Permanent base: https://w3id.org/digest-law/us/

Identifiers

  • Digest bundle — https://w3id.org/digest-law/us/ { area}/ { topic}/
  • Retained source — https://w3id.org/digest-law/us/ { area}/ { topic}/sources/ { slug}/
  • Audit trail — https://w3id.org/digest-law/us/ { area}/ { topic}/audit/

What's in a bundle

Each bundle is one research run on one legal issue: the digest with inline citations, the retained source documents in full (free public legal authorities only — CourtListener, GovInfo, eCFR, Cornell LII, court and agency sites; never a proprietary database), and the machine-readable audit trail tying every claim to a retained source.

Status

The identifier namespace and this landing page are live. The full browsable site — per-bundle pages, search, source viewers — is in preparation (Astro static build, working domain digest.law). Until it ships, identifier URLs resolve here.

Topics are organised under the Open Legal Issue Taxonomy (OLIT), the companion SKOS vocabulary by the same maintainer.
