## Sources & provenance

- Every digest bundle retains its sources **in full, as fetched**, alongside a
  machine-readable audit trail recording what was searched, what was retained,
  and which passages support the digest.
- Every bundle satisfies an **evidence floor of at least 2 retained sources**;
  bundles that could not be grounded were purged from the corpus (2026-07-28)
  rather than published thin.
- **No content from any proprietary legal database** (Westlaw, Lexis, Bloomberg
  Law, or similar) is used at any stage; this exclusion is an enforced rule of
  the research pipeline.
- Digests are **machine-generated and machine-reviewed**. The audit trail exists
  precisely so that no statement need be taken on the generator's authority:
  every claim is traceable to a retained public source.

## Not legal advice

The digests are research summaries of public legal sources, produced by an
automated pipeline. They may be incomplete, out of date, or wrong. They are
**not legal advice**, and no attorney–client relationship is created by reading
them. Consult the retained primary sources — and a licensed attorney — before
relying on anything here.
